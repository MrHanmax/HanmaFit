    A note from the founder

    Every calculator I sent clients to gave them one confident number, and most of those numbers were wrong. Not by a little. By enough to make a plan that was working look like a failure by week three.

    So I built the one I wanted to hand them. It tells you how sure it is. It measures your maintenance instead of guessing it. And it treats losing fat, building muscle and doing both at once as different jobs, because they are.

    Scope

    Equations estimate; they do not measure. Resting rate varies by about 200 kcal either side of any prediction, which is why every figure carries a range and why two weeks of your own logs beat any further calculation. This is a nutrition plan, not a medical prescription.
